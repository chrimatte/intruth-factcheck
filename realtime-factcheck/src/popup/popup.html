Before you start

Data transfer
  • Tab audio is sent directly to Deepgram for live transcription.
  • Transcripts and claims are sent to Anthropic for analysis.
  • Claim search queries are sent to Serper to find evidence.

Keys are stored locally in Chrome. Saving a key does not validate it; each provider accepts or rejects it when a request starts.

Using these APIs may incur charges on your provider accounts. InTruth does not control provider pricing or limits.
